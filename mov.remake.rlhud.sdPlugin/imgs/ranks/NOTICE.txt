Rank icons (bronze-1.png ... supersonic-legend.png, unranked.png)

These are the rank icons of Rocket League. Rocket League and its artwork are (c) Psyonix LLC / Epic Games, Inc.
They were taken from the Rocket League Wiki (https://rocketleague.fandom.com), reduced to 96x96 px, and are used only to
show the player's own rank in this free, unofficial, fan-made Stream Deck plugin.

They are NOT covered by the licence of this repository (see LICENSE, which applies to the plugin's own code and artwork).
The plugin is not affiliated with or endorsed by Psyonix, Epic Games or Elgato. If the rights holder asks for these files to
be removed, they will be removed; the plugin then falls back to its own built-in emblems.

To use different icons, put PNG files with the same names into %APPDATA%\RLHUD\rank-icons\ - files there win over these.
